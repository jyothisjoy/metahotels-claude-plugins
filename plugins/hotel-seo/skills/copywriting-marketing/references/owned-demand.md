# Owned demand: internal plays and guest retention

Sources: Hormozi, Gym Launch ch 15, 16, 21, 26 (note 24); $100M Leads, referrals (note 03); LTV and Retention (note 09); Agora list economics and reactivation (note 18). **[MH]** marks adaptations, which are inference.

**Why it's here.** Paid Search mostly captures demand that already exists (the 24 Oct 2026 backfill: almost all Search spend was brand queries). The cheapest new demand comes from the base the hotel already owns: past guests, members, local residents and in-house guests. There's no ad spend, the trust is already there, and every booking is direct. Agora's whole business is the list. Treat the hotel's guest list the same way.

**Gates first:**
- Opt-in lists only (category: regulatory or platform-policy).
- Real capacity and real dates.
- Offers match the live rate and the hotel's parity and OTA contracts (contractual).
- Hotel management approves every offer.
- Check the booking engine and CRM can actually do the mechanic (Mirai capability is often unverified).

## Internal plays (Gym Launch ch 26) [MH]
**An internal play = a time-limited campaign to people who already know the hotel, built on a hook** (something new that sparks curiosity or desire).
- **Cadence:** about every 6 weeks, each running 2–4 weeks.
- **Audiences:** past guests (12–24 months), members, local residents, in-house guests.
- **Hook types:**
  - **Experience or community** (easiest to deliver): a "Staycation Supper Club", a Ramadan Suhoor series, "bring a friend: second room half price", team-building Fridays.
  - **Wellness** (higher price, best for upselling): a "7-day reset" spa programme, a residents' pool pass.
  - **Occasion:** anniversaries, birthdays, school-holiday kids' clubs.
- **Seasonal "why now"** answers urgency honestly: Eid, the UAE National Day weekend, summer in the city, F1, school holidays. Dates are verified on the day.
- **An end event as the deadline:** a closing dinner, a pool party, a chef's table. People holding tickets for a date don't drift away.
- **Marketing it:**
  - a 7–10 day countdown of short, fun staff videos ("advertainment") on WhatsApp, Instagram and email;
  - each message points to the next;
  - a friend price or friend spot for outsiders (about a third of sign-ups can come from outside the base).
- **Measure:** take-up rate as a share of the list (Hormozi treats it as a gauge of goodwill), revenue per participant, and the share who move up to the premium tier afterwards.
- **Stack:** the play → an add-on bundle → the premium tier or membership.

## Guest life cycle: Connect → Testify → Refer → Ascend (Gym Launch ch 15) [MH]
- **Connect:** the first impression decides how forgiving guests are later. Pre-arrival WhatsApp from a named person; instant confirmation; directions that actually help.
- **Testify, done the compliant way:**
  - ask for reviews framed as inspiration ("your story helps the next family choose");
  - ask right after a great moment (check-out, a compliment to staff);
  - **never incentivise or require reviews** on Google, TripAdvisor or Booking. Their policies prohibit it (category: platform-policy). The gym book's "ethical bribe" doesn't transfer.
- **Refer:** a bring-a-friend offer, or a real gift for the friend (named, with an expiry), not for the review.
- **Ascend:** the pre-arrival prescriptive upsell (`offers-money-models.md`), the member programme, the next stay.

## Arrival and stay standard ("hamburger", Gym Launch ch 21) [MH]
Offer it to the hotel as the guest-ownership pillar's service script, not as copy:
- greet by name within 10 seconds of arrival;
- introduce the property;
- **two moments of personal recognition per stay**;
- a clear "what's on today";
- a departure moment with the ask (rebook, refer, review) handed over, not emailed later.

## Guest five horsemen (Gym Launch ch 16) [MH]
- **A post-stay personal message** within 48 hours that doesn't feel automated. "Systematise, never automate": once guests sense automation, they stop reading.
- **A lapse trigger:** no return within the guest's usual cycle (e.g. 12 months for an annual family trip), then a reason to return.
- **Milestone notes that ask nothing:** stay anniversaries and birthdays where consented. Every other note may carry one ask, in the P.S.
- **Returning-guest events** a few times a year: they generate content and testimonials (Marketing Machine).
- **Departure feedback handled as a conversation**, not just a survey.

## Offer timing for guests (Hormozi workshop, note 27 §1) [MH]
Offer the extra early: at confirmation, about 24 hours after booking, at pre-arrival, at check-in, mid-stay. Four of his five upsell moments are in the first half, because attention falls away. Don't wait for checkout.

## Copy for these pieces
Copy for these pieces uses the normal brief, platform and gates (SKILL.md). Messages are short, with one offer, one action and a real deadline, and identify the sender in the first line. Results go in the swipe file like any campaign (platform = email / WhatsApp / Instagram).
