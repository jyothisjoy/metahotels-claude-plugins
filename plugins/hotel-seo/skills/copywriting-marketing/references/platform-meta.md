# Meta (Facebook and Instagram): platform playbook

Limits and benchmarks are **inherited** knowledge as of mid-2026, not verified this session. Check Meta's current specs at build time. Meta campaigns are built by Saif from the brief. The `meta-ads` MCP is read-only here: its write tools are denied in the folder settings. Pull results and tracking with it (`data-brief.md` → Meta data).

## How Meta differs from Google
Google captures demand that already exists (they searched). Meta **interrupts**: the reader wasn't looking. So the hook does the job the search query does on Google. Hormozi's ad assembly line (hook 80% of the effort) and the Agora lead types for less aware readers apply most here. Under broad targeting and Advantage+, **the creative is the targeting**: the call-out in the first line or first second picks the audience.

## Copy fields
- **Primary text:** about 125 characters show before "See more". The hook and the one benefit go in that space. Long copy can follow for readers who expand, and story leads often win on Meta.
- **Headline:** about 40 characters; truncates sooner on some placements. Lead with the offer or outcome.
- **Description:** short, often hidden. Never put anything essential there.
- One CTA button, matched to the action (Book Now / Learn More).

## Creative
- Formats: 1:1 and 4:5 for feed; 9:16 for Stories and Reels (keep text and faces out of the top and bottom safe zones).
- **Video: the first 3 seconds are the hook.** Open on the payoff (the pool at sunset, the family laughing), not the logo. Captions burned in, because most viewing is sound-off.
- Native beats polished: phone-shot staff videos, real guest UGC (with permission), a room walk-through. Use the Scrambler's image and video banks (`channel-playbooks.md`).
- Make 3–5 creatives per angle, each differing in **one** variable (hook line, opening shot or format).
- **Also test genuinely new concepts, not only tweaks.** Hormozi (note 27 §11) says Meta's Andromeda update favours net-new creative over small variations. His reading; check current Meta guidance at build time.

## Measurement
- **Hook rate** = 3-second plays ÷ impressions. **Hold rate** = ThruPlays ÷ 3-second plays. Low hook rate → fix the opening; low hold rate → fix the middle; good both with low CTR → fix the offer or CTA.
- Watch frequency and CPM for fatigue.
- **UTMs on every ad** (source, medium, campaign, content = ad name) so GA4 and Mirai can judge Meta on bookings, not platform-attributed purchases.
- Same sample gate as everywhere: no verdict below ~1,000 link clicks (CLAUDE.md rule 2). Meta's learning phase also needs about 50 optimisation events a week per ad set (Meta's figure, inherited); on small budgets, optimise for a higher-funnel event or consolidate ad sets.

## Policy (category: platform-policy)
- **No personal-attribute call-outs**: "Are you stressed?" or "Divorced?" asserts a personal characteristic and breaks Meta's rules. Call out the situation, not the person ("Planning an Eid break with the kids?").
- No before/after body images or health claims.
- Prices and offers must match the landing page.
- Text in images is allowed but keep it light; the image should work on its own.
